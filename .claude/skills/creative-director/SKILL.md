## Overview

Creative direction means defining a visual language that is coherent, purposeful, and appropriate for the audience. Every visual choice should serve the design brief.

## Direction Elements

- **Mood**: What should the user feel? (professional, playful, luxurious, minimal)
- **Color Palette**: Primary, secondary, accent, neutral, semantic (success/warning/error)
- **Typography**: Heading font, body font, scale, hierarchy
- **Spacing**: Density — airy vs. compact
- **Imagery**: Photography style, illustration style, iconography
- **Motion**: Subtle vs. bold animations, transition style

## Rules

- Every direction choice must have a reason — "it looks cool" isn't one
- Consistency within a direction is more important than individual elements
- Document the direction so others can apply it without you
