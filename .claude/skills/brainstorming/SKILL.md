## Overview

Brainstorming is divergent thinking — generate options before converging on one. Quantity over quality at first, then filter.

## Process

### Diverge (Generate)
- Produce 3-5 distinct design directions
- Each direction should have a clear concept/mood
- Don't self-censor — wild ideas can spark practical ones

### Converge (Filter)
- Evaluate each option against the design brief
- Identify the 1-2 strongest directions
- Combine ideas where sensible

## Output Format

```markdown
### Direction 1: <Name>
- **Concept:** <one-line idea>
- **Mood:** <emotional quality>
- **Reference:** <inspiration or precedent>
- **Why it works:** <rationale>
```

## Rules

- No coding during brainstorming — this is thinking time
- If all your ideas look similar, you're not diverging enough
