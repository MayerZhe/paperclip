## Overview

Design consultation means evaluating a design against principles and goals, not personal taste. Every piece of feedback should have a reason grounded in usability, accessibility, or brand.

## Consultation Format

```markdown
### What Works
- <element>: <why it works>

### What Could Improve
- <element>: <issue> → <suggestion> (because <reason>)

### Questions to Consider
- <open question about intent or constraints>
```

## Rules

- Always explain WHY — "this button should be blue" is useless without reasoning
- Consider the user's context, not your preference
- If something is a matter of taste, say so
