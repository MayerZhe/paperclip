## Overview

A design brief translates requirements into design direction. It answers: who is this for, what problem does it solve, what constraints exist, what does success look like.

## Brief Structure

```markdown
## Design Brief: <Project>

### Audience
- Who will use this?
- What is their context/device?

### Problem
- What problem does this solve?

### Constraints
- Brand guidelines, existing design system, platform requirements

### Success Criteria
- How do we know the design works?
```

## Rules
- Brief before visuals — always
- If the brief isn't clear, the design won't be either
- Reference existing design tokens and components when possible
